About

Streaks

Streaks is a privacy-first, offline habit tracker. Habit names, completions, notes and statistics are stored in IndexedDB on your device.

No analytics, account or cloud sync is required. Optional encrypted WebDAV backup is user-initiated.

Built with HTML5, CSS3, Vanilla JavaScript, IndexedDB and a Service Worker.
